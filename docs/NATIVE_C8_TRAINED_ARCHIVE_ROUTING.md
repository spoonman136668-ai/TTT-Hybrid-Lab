# C8 — Actually trained P7 factual-history routing over saved C6 native memories (DEV)

## Source and distinct problem

C6 proved that **old native fast memory** still reduces old-task error when restored into the current trained model, but the test knew the correct archive in advance. C7 qualified the **existing P6 fixed sign-of-causal-effect rule** as an observed-history selector: on six familiar development scenarios, the hand-coded rule reached 100% late-block route accuracy, matching a labeled oracle only after observing factual outcomes. That does not prove the selector can be **learned by gradients** or that it beats a competent trained conventional approach.

C8 bridges independent research [P7 PR #62](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/TTT-Hybrid-Lab/pull/62) to the C7/C6 genuine native saved checkpoints. The original P7 training script and frozen protocol were copied **byte-for-byte** from `7658be2cd4ff66afb3fb46519b516d8071641a36` (Git blobs `7db1fe42695f16bf2afe730f4348210156ac3c24`, `1ca3b8fa6b9f425bb4aea35462084c7e1fb5530e`). The original research branch is untouched. C8 does not create a rival learning mechanism or retune the independently developed P7 architecture.

## Frozen pre-execution design

`experiments/native_c8_trained_history_archive_dev.json` fixes:

1. Training P7 `LearnedRouter` (828 parameters) and a separate conventional `ConventionalMLP` (849 parameters) using **the unchanged P7 `synthetic_dev()`**: 6,000 public factual training examples, 1,920 separate-RNG DEV validation examples, 8 prior completed causal (action, observed consequence) pairs; exactly **600 AdamW updates** of **256 examples** per trained model with the original P7 seed-matched minibatch sampler. Train three independent source model seeds `303,307,311`. Actual weights and model/optimizer update receipts required. No context labels are inputs or gradient targets. Parameter count differs by 2.5%, **but independently matched FLOPs, total training cost and fully converged stability are not claimed**. The original P7 research reported the conventional MLP beating the P7 mixture; this negative is preserved, and C8 does not presume it reverses.
2. In parallel, unchanged original P3 factual native training from seeds `46111,46117,46129`: train 40 old, then 40 reversed factual prequential AdamW updates. Save immutable root→40→80 P2 checkpoints, cold-restore separate C2 candidates, verify source ancestry, cursor, protected champion and stored recurrent/fast/history. All **native** learned model weights, optimizer state and saved memory become **read-only** before evaluation.
3. Two original C7 known public analytic orders `old/recent/old/recent` and `recent/old/recent/old`, **16 events per block, 64 events per trial**. Current sensor zero, elapsed 0.2 and alternating actions are identical across contexts. Use a fixed 8-pair history buffer of **only past completed factual consequences**. For the first 8 events, trained/negative selectors receive an explicit same old-memory tie break and are not evaluated on an unseen history prefix.
4. For every candidate context decision, evaluate both **model-predicted** hypothetical action consequences `a=+1` and `a=-1` with the same preceding history. Select the old or recent native memory from the **sign of their predicted causal slope**. Both P7 and conventional MLP receive the same two-forward-call interface. This is predicted counterfactual *output*, not a counterfactual outcome used for training. The current event's real outcome is computed only **after all routes and factual predictions**, then appended to history. No hidden context label, phase index or current target enters the selector.
5. Seven evaluation arms: **trained P7 learned router**, **fully trained conventional MLP**, original **P6 fixed factual slope rule**, **oracle context-label upper bound**, **always recent**, same trained P7 with **reversed eight-pair historical order**, and same P7 with **all-zero historical input**. P6, oracle and always-recent *must match the unchanged C7 evaluator's complete 64-event route/error vectors bit-for-bit* on each platform, not just approximate aggregate scores.
6. Calculate immediate window (events 0–3 in each block) and late window (events 4–15) error, route accuracy, raw per-event ledger, per-seed model/optimizer/scoring provenance and hash. A result of **engineering integrity PASS** is independent of the actual trained P7 versus MLP comparison: **null/negative outcomes are preserved**, not optimized after observation.

The preregistered diagnostics (allowed to fail individually) are trained P7 late accuracy at least 0.75, lower late MSE than always-recent by at least 0.10, lower than **fully trained MLP** by at least 0.01, lower than chronology-reversed history by at least 0.05, lower than zero-history by at least 0.10, and within 0.05 MSE of the hidden-label oracle. No threshold changes after evaluation.

## What this cannot establish

The original P7 architecture includes explicitly initialized **positive/negative signed-effect experts** and handcrafted eight-event history, and training data share a familiar synthetic algebraic family. It is a genuine learned weighting, but **not token-free JEPA, autonomous reasoning, unfamiliar transfer, independently validated scientific retention, or recursive self-improvement**. The two checkpoint memories are supplied by the evaluator at design time; the trained P7 module is **not integrated into the live native model or trained end-to-end**. A context cannot be known before sufficient observed history. The original P7 fixed 600-step protocol showed a conventional MLP win and unstable plateau, so even a good C8 downstream archive-selection result does not erase that research negative.

The P7 and conventional arms have equal source examples, actions, update counts, minibatch indices, near-matched trainable parameter counts and two inference forwards per decision; they **do not** have independently FLOP-matched training costs, identical inductive priors or demonstrated full convergence. The heuristic P6 arm is a useful untrained negative, **not** compute-matched. No novel sealed scientific worlds, frozen S26/S27 seeds, official first-use, owner credentials, encrypted off-host checkpoint store, accepted/latest pointer, autonomous training/merge/promotion, FORGE delegation or new queue/controller/runner is allowed.
